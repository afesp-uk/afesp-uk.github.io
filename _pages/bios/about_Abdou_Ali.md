<p style="text-align: justify;">
Dr. Abdou Ali is a senior expert in hydrometeorology and climate, currently serving as Head of the Climate–Water–Meteorology Department at the AGRHYMET Regional Climate Centre for West Africa and the Sahel (AGRHYMET RCC-WAS), based in Niamey, Niger. In this strategic position, he leads several regional initiatives in climate services, water resources management, and early warning systems, aimed at strengthening the resilience of populations to climate and hydrometeorological risks.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Holding a PhD from the École Nationale Polytechnique de Grenoble (France) and a State Engineering Degree from the École Nationale Polytechnique d’Alger (Algeria), and having completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the École Nationale Polytechnique de Paris (Paris) in France as part of the Megha-Tropiques satellite mission, Dr. Abdou Ali has developed strong scientific and operational expertise, recognized at both regional and international levels.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Author of more than 35 scientific publications in international peer-reviewed journals, he actively contributes to advancing knowledge in climate, hydrology, and hydrometeorological services in West Africa. He has advanced expertise in statistics and geostatistics
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
In 2004, Dr. Abdou Ali was awarded the START Young Scientist Award by the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP) in recognition of his research on climate in the Sahel.
</p>
