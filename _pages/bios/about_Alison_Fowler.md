I am a National Centre for Earth Observation Research Fellow based at the University of Reading, specialising in data assimilation theory and its application to the Earth sciences. Over the past 15 years, I have had the opportunity to contribute to a diverse range of projects, often in close collaboration with the Met Office and ECMWF. My research interests include quantifying the impact of observations on forecast skill to inform the design of observing networks; accounting for observational uncertainty to maximise the information extracted from observations; and correcting systematic model and observational errors. I am particularly passionate about translating these theoretical challenges into real-world applications, including numerical weather prediction and marine ecology forecasting. I currently supervise two AFESP projects: Rishabh Bhatt’s work on developing algorithms to account for spatially correlated observation errors, and Kate Huxtable’s research on adaptive localisation.

Before joining the University of Reading to undertake a PhD in Meteorology, I completed a BSc in Mathematics and an MRes in Physics of the Earth and Atmosphere at the University of Leeds.
