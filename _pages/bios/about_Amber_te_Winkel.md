<p style="text-align: justify;">
Amber is currently pursuing a PhD under the supervision of Hilary Weller (University of Reading), Christian Kühnlein (ECMWF), and James Kent (Met Office) on the numerics for advection at the University of Reading. Before this, she did a Bachelor's in Physics and Environmental Science at the University of Amsterdam, worked for climate foundation Urgenda, and did a Master's in Physics and Meteorology at LMU Munich, with a dissertation on cloud microphysics and turbulence supervised by Prof. Fabian Hoffmann.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Her interests lie in numerical mathematics, fluid dynamics, computational physics, and machine learning applied to weather and climate modelling. She is currently investigating how to improve numerical methods for advection with adaptively implicit-explicit time stepping. This approach removes the stability limit of Eulerian explicit finite-volume approaches, allowing for conservative and stable time integration with long time steps on arbitrary grids. Amber has successfully increased the accuracy of this approach for long time steps. She has implemented and tested her science in LFRic, the new Met Office model, and will do so even more extensively in PMAP, the new ECMWF model.
</p>
