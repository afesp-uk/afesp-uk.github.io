<p style="text-align: justify;">
My name is Andrew Kenny and I am a first-year PhD student investigating the connection between the late-spring stratospheric polar vortex and subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) predictability of the Summer North Atlantic Oscillation. I'm interested in understanding the physical processes that link modes of climate variability (teleconnections) and using them to improve S2S forecasting, especially during the summer, when S2S forecasting is more challenging.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
My supervisory team consists of Xiaocen Shen (University of Reading), Chris O'Reilly (University of Reading), Nick Dunstone (Met Office), and Steffen Tietsche (ECMWF).
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Before starting my PhD, I obtained my B.S in Meteorology from the University of Oklahoma, and M.Sc in Applied Meteorology and Climate from the University of Reading.
</p>
