<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am a Professor of Environmental Physics at the Department of Geography and Environmental Science, UoR.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 0;">Research Interests:</p>
<ul style="display: flow-root;">
  <li>Interactions in the soil-vegetation-atmosphere system; implications for land-atmosphere feedbacks</li>
  <li>Development of improved below-ground process descriptions in Land Surface models, with a focus on soil water and heat (fluxes, stores, and hydraulic and thermal properties).</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I have been a member of academic staff at UoR since 1999. Over the years, I have built up a large portfolio of research grants broadly in the context of water-, food- and energy security, and related impacts of weather extremes (flooding, droughts, heatwaves). I have been an executive board member of the International Soil Modelling Consortium – since 2017, and I co-chaired the international GEWEX (Global Energy and Water Cycle Exchanges) GLASS (Global Land Atmosphere System Study) panel between 2021-2025. Within GLASS I am currently still actively involved in the <a href="https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/bams/107/3/BAMS-D-26-0003.1.xml">SoilWat</a> activity, that aims to improve subsurface processes in land surface models. I am the PI of a 5-year AFESP research grant called <a href="https://research.reading.ac.uk/earth-system-prediction/opportunities/research-projects/">VaaSS</a>: ‘Vegetation as a Soil Sensor’; towards a high-fidelity Integrated Forecasting System via ground-breaking and ambitious data-assimilation of the dynamic soil-vegetation hydraulic continuum. Dr Rajsekhar Kandala is the researcher on this project.
</p>
