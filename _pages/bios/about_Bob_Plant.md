<p style="text-align: justify;">
I'm Bob Plant, a professor at the Department of Meteorology and the University of Reading. My role within AFESP is as PhD supervisor for <a href="https://afesp-uk.github.io/projects/phd_kaustubh_mittal/">Kaustubh Mittal</a> and <a href="https://afesp-uk.github.io/projects/phd_ken_rui_fong/">Ken Rui Fong</a>, working on medium-range forecast busts and on the prospects for multi-scale parameterization.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
More broadly, I am interested in moist convection and boundary layer turbulence, particularly their effects on mesoscale and larger-scale flows and their appropriate representation in models at various scales. To see more about my work in these areas, you can look at <a href="https://www.met.reading.ac.uk/~sws00rsp/home/">my webpage</a>.
</p>
