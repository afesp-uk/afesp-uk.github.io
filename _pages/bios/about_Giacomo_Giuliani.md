Hi!

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I'm a 2nd-year PhD student in the AFESP-DTP at the University of Reading, studying the mechanisms of tropical cyclone formation in the Southern Indian Ocean. My work investigates how tropical cyclones and seeds interact with the Intertropical Convergence Zone, and uses km-scale coupled regional models to address this question. More generally, I'm interested in how the large-scale circulation affects weather events in the Tropics, and what benefits km-scale coupled models bring to representing these processes.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
My supervisors are Pier Luigi Vidale (University of Reading, NCAS), Chris Holloway (University of Reading), Elizabeth Kendon (Met Office) and Malcolm Roberts (Met Office).
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Before starting my PhD, I attained a BSc in Physics and an MSc in Science of Climate from the University of Bologna, and spent nine months as a researcher at the Euro-Mediterranean Centre for Climate Change, where I developed clustering methods to improve seasonal tropical cyclone forecasts.
</p>
