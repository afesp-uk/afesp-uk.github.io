<p style="text-align: justify;">
Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher on the AFESP project <a href="/projects/1_project/">ECFoundLand: The ECMWF Land Surface Hydrology Foundation Model</a>. I am also a Marie Curie Postdoctoral Fellow affiliated with the University of Reading, the University of Oxford and the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF), and I work with <a href="#hannah-cloke">Prof. Hannah Cloke</a>. My research combines artificial intelligence with satellite Earth Observation to improve hydrological prediction and flood forecasting.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Before joining Reading, I gained research experience at the National Research Council of Italy, the European Space Agency and the University of California, Irvine. At the Research Institute for Geo-Hydrological Protection of the National Research Council of Italy (IRPI-CNR), I was a Postdoctoral Researcher with Prof. Luca Brocca. There I developed HR-PrecipNet, a machine learning framework for 1-km satellite precipitation estimation, and SM2RAIN-Climate, a long-term global rainfall dataset derived from satellite soil moisture. I also contributed to several European research projects, including Digital Twin Earth (DTE) Hydrology Evolution, 4DHydro, 4D MED-Hydrology, extrAIm and Open-Earth-Monitor Cyberinfrastructure (OEMC). You can find my publications on <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=A1x2BaMAAAAJ&amp;hl=en">Google Scholar</a> and LinkedIn.
</p>
