<p style="text-align: justify;">
Hi! I am a PhD student in the AFESP programme at the University of Reading, supervised by Prof. Maarten Ambaum. I am interested in the dynamics of mid-latitude storm tracks, particularly the nonlinear interaction between baroclinicity and storm activity, and how this interaction contributes to storm track variability and medium-range predictability. I am also interested in how reduced-order dynamical models can be used to understand systematic biases in weather forecast models.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I obtained my BSc in Earth System Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and my MSc in Atmospheric and Climate Sciences from ETH Zürich. During my MSc, I investigated the predictability of potential-vorticity dynamics and extreme rainfall in European flood events using reanalysis and ensemble forecast data.
</p>
