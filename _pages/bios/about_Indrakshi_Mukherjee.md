<p style="text-align: justify;">
Hello! I started my PhD at the University of Reading in October 2023. Prior to this, I completed a master’s degree in Atmosphere and Ocean Sciences and a bachelor’s degree in Geology in India.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Having grown up in India, I became fascinated by how the South Asian monsoon affects human life in contrasting ways. Farmers eagerly await abundant rainfall before sowing their crops, whilst the same rains can cause disruptions in urban areas. This highlights the importance of accurate monsoon forecasting.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
My PhD project focuses on subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) prediction of the South Asian monsoon. In particular, I study the Boreal Summer Intraseasonal Oscillation (BSISO), which is associated with intraseasonal fluctuations in rainfall. Using observational and model datasets, I investigate how large-scale climate drivers, such as the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), influence BSISO dynamics and the underlying physical mechanisms. I also evaluate how well the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) S2S model captures these relationships. In addition, I examine whether BSISO prediction skill varies with the ENSO state. This research could help pave the way for conditional BSISO forecasts based on the ENSO mean state.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">

</p>
