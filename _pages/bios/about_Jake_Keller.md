<p style="text-align: justify;">
Jake Keller is a third-year PhD student at the University of Reading, focusing on mid-latitude atmospheric dynamics and predictability. His research explores how small- and mesoscale processes, such as convection and moist dynamics, influence synoptic-scale dynamics and their representation in numerical weather prediction.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
His current work investigates the predictability of high-impact weather, including the role of extratropical transition and European blocking in high-impact weather events. His broader interests include Rossby wave dynamics, ensemble forecasting, and cross-scale interactions.
</p>
