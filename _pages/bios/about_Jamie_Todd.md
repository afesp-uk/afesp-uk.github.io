<p style="text-align: justify;">
Hello! I started my PhD in September 2026, supervised by: Prof Andrew Turner (National Centre for Atmospheric Science; NCAS); Dr Akshay Deoras (NCAS); Dr Eliza Karlowska (NCAS) and Dr Richard Jones (Met Office). The project revolves around the representation of the Indian and West African monsoons in global km-scale simulations. I am interested in how small-scale features like orographic circulations and land-sea breezes — which are better resolved at km-scale — modulate the monsoon diurnal cycle, and how improvements in representing monsoon rainfall can have upscale impact on the large-scale circulation. A key focus of the project later on will be investigating the interaction of monsoons with other modes of tropical variability, such as the Madden-Julian Oscillation, and how this changes with horizontal resolution and choice of representation of convection.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Before starting my PhD, I did my undergraduate degree in Geography at the University of Oxford from 2022-25 during which I specialized in climate science. I then undertook an MSc in Applied Meteorology and Climate at the University of Reading, with my dissertation exploring the characteristics of organized convection in the West African Sahel in global km-scale simulations.
</p>
