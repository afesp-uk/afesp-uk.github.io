<p style="text-align: justify;">
Magdalena A. Balmaseda is a visiting professor at the School of Mathematics, Physics and Computational Science at the University of Reading. She retired from ECMWF in October 2025, after 30 years of service in the Predictability Section in the Research Department, which she led since 2016.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
She received her PhD in Physics at the Universidad de Alcalá de Henares, Madrid, Spain. After a post-doctoral position at Oxford University on predictability and variability of El Niño Southern Oscillation, she joined ECMWF as a member of the team in experimental seasonal forecasts. Her work has contributed to the consolidation of the ocean and sea ice components in the ECMWF seamless prediction systems. She has promoted ocean and coupled reanalyses activities for initialization of coupled forecasts and for understanding of climate signals. Her research interests cover a wide range of areas, including forecasting system design, predictability of weather and climate, probabilistic forecasting, and scale interactions in the climate system. She has developed her career by helping to understand weather and climate links using numerical models and observations. In doing that, she has contributed to build bridges between communities: weather and climate, observationalists and modellers, oceanographic and atmospheric scientists.
</p>
