Regina R. Rodrigues is Associate Professor of Physical Oceanography and Climate at the Federal University of Santa Catarina, Brazil. Her research examines how tropical ocean basins interact to drive extremes far beyond the tropics, and how those extremes compound: drought with land and marine heatwaves, and marine heatwaves with high acidity and low productivity. She investigates how changes in Atlantic circulation propagate into South American rainfall, tropical Atlantic reefs and the ocean carbon sink, bringing a physical oceanography perspective to questions usually framed by ecologists and atmospheric scientists. Regina has held leadership roles in international scientific assessments of climate (Reviewer Editor and Lead Author of the IPCC AR), in the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP), and in the International Association for the Physical Sciences of the Oceans (IAPSO). She has been Editor of the Nature Portfolio journal Communications Earth &amp; Environment since 2020. In Brazil, she holds a chair on the Interministerial Committee of Climate Change, advising the Federal Government.
</p>
