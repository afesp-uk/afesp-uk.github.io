<p style="text-align: justify;">
My background is in physics, and I hold a PhD in condensed matter theoretical/computational physics. Since working at the University of Reading my work has been in inverse problems, applied to meteorological science. Data assimilation (or DA) is a powerful means of solving the inverse problem, which is the process of combining diverse observations with models to help infer quantitative knowledge about the Earth system. I work with a variety of DA techniques, including variational and ensemble methods. I work mainly (but not exclusively) on atmospheric DA (especially for convective-scale systems), and in surface flux estimation. Many of my studies have focused on modelling the ‘B-matrix’, which helps quantify errors in the prior knowledge that is essential for DA.
</p>
