<p style="text-align: justify;">
I work in the Physical Processes Team within ECMWF’s Research Department. After completing my PhD at the Max Planck Institute for Meteorology in Hamburg, I joined ECMWF in 2020 as an Alexander von Humboldt Fellow, focusing on improving the representation of moist physical processes, particularly deep convection, in the Integrated Forecasting System (IFS). I subsequently worked on model developments to enable the IFS to run on decadal timescales at km-scale resolutions, first within the nextGEMS project and later in Destination Earth. In 2024, I returned to numerical weather prediction (NWP) timescales, and since then, my main focus has been on improving the representation of convection in the operational IFS.
</p>
