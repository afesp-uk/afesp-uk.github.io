**PhD Student**: Amber te Winkel

**Description**: The most accurate and efficient models of the global atmosphere can take long, stable and accurate time steps using semi-Lagrangian advection. Interpolating semi-Lagrangian advection does not conserve mass or any other transported quantity. Flux-form semi-Lagrangian is restricted to structured grids such as latitude-longitude grids and the cubed sphere. This project is creating adaptively implicit/explicit transport schemes that are stable and accurate on arbitrary meshes of the sphere. Accuracy is achieved by only using implicit time stepping where needed and efficiency is achieved by tailoring the implicit matrix solver to the transport problem. Adaptively implicit/explicit transport enables increases in local resolution without the need to reduce the time step.
