**Supervisor**: Oscar Martinez-Alvarado

**PhD Student**: Jake Keller

**Description**: Numerical weather prediction centres are striving to develop global models at higher resolutions. As resolution increases, processes that were sub-grid scale, such as convection, are able to be partially resolved by the model, and parametrizations can be relaxed. Though mesoscale processes can be more accurately represented by these models, biases introduced to synoptic scale are yet to be fully understood. This project aims to understand these biases by investigating feedback between mesoscale and large-scale processes that will change as model resolution increases.
