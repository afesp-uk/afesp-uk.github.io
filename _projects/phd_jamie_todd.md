**Supervisor**: Andrew Turner

**PhD Student**: Jamie Todd

**Description**: Monsoons are a critical source of water for over two-thirds of the world’s population, but their representation in global climate models suffers as a result of the models’ coarse horizontal resolution and treatment of sub-grid scale processes. New state-of-the-art global simulations run at km-scale (≤ 10 km horizontal grid spacing) offer an opportunity to better represent processes key to simulating monsoons — such as the diurnal cycle of rainfall and its coupling with small-scale circulations — and the impact of these processes on the larger-scale tropical circulation. This project investigates whether monsoon behaviour improves, and whether the nature of monsoon interaction with the general circulation changes, at the km-scale when compared to simulations run at coarser resolution.
