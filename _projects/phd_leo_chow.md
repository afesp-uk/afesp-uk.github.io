**Supervisor**: Xiangbo Feng

**PhD Student**: Leo Chow

The accurate prediction of tropical cyclone genesis by numerical modeling two weeks ahead remains a challenge. Equatorial waves can promote or discurage tropical cyclone genesis depending on their strengths and phases. Since these waves have a period within sub-seasonal timescale, they may provide sources of predictability for tropical cyclone genesis forecast. This project seeks to understand the relationship between equatorial waves and tropical cyclone genesis through the use of observational analysis, numerical modelong and idealized experiments. We will also evaluate the prediction skills and predictability of tropical cyclone genesis in relation to equatorial waves in the ECMWF and Met Office sub-seasonal hindcasts.
