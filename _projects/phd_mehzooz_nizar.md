**Supervisor**: Reinhard Schiemann

**PhD Student**: Mehzooz Nizar

**Description**: Global AI Weather Prediction (AIWP) models are showing strong potential to reshape weather forecasting, but their skill must be assessed from a meteorological perspective before they can be used operationally. This PhD project evaluates how well AIWP models forecast monsoon weather, a demanding test case because of its strong spatial and temporal variability and the high forecast uncertainty on seasonal and sub-seasonal timescales.
