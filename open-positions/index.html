AFESP PhD studentships – 2027 call

Applications now open · Closes midnight GMT, Tuesday 12 January 2027 · Up to ten fully funded studentships · Up to four years · Starting autumn 2027 · Open worldwide

The AFESP Doctoral Training Programme at the University of Reading is offering up to ten new PhD studentships to start in autumn 2027. Projects fit one of three themes:

  1. Sub-seasonal Earth system prediction (up to one month)
  2. Kilometre-scale Earth system simulation
  3. Data assimilation across scales

Timeline: panel interviews in the week commencing 13 February 2027, then project matching and supervisor interviews, with final offers by April 2027.

How to apply: complete the online form on the PhD opportunities page, including a personal statement on your motivation, relevant skills, preferred AFESP theme(s) and expected societal impact. Questions: Claire Wyllie, AFESP Programme Manager, AFESP-DTP@reading.ac.uk.

Urban climate, micrometeorology and boundary-layer research positions

Advertised 3 November 2026 · Full-time or part-time · A wide range of levels · Related project: URBANE

Within a broad range of urban climate, micrometeorology and boundary-layer projects, we have a few positions available for people with different skill sets. Do you have experience or good skills in one or more of the following?

  1. Running (1) the UM, (2) the IFS or (3) other equivalent atmospheric models
  2. Analysing UM, IFS or other equivalent model output
  3. Analysing observations (e.g. DWL, LAS, ALC, IRT or EC sensors)
  4. Writing code and making code more efficient (Fortran or Python)
  5. Handling and processing GIS data (e.g. to create databases, model input or evaluation data)
  6. Running land surface models
  7. Analysing land surface model output (e.g. evaluation against observations)
  8. Developing land surface models (e.g. code enhancements, model coupling, integration of new sub-models)

Examples of recent papers covering some of these topics:

  • (1), (2): Glazer et al. (2025), Saunders et al. (2024), Hall et al. (2024)
  • (3): Looschelders et al. (2025), Morrison et al. (2025), Fenner et al. (2024)
  • (5): Hertwig et al. (2025)
  • (6), (7), (8): Liu et al. (2024), Xie et al. (2024), Xie et al. (2023)

How to apply: if you have one or more of these skills and would like to know more, please contact Prof Sue Grimmond at c.s.grimmond@reading.ac.uk. Positions could be full-time or part-time and could be at a wide range of levels.

UKRI Future Leaders Fellowships (Round 12)

UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), with internal selection at the University of Reading

Reading internal call open since October 2026 · Closes 12:00 noon, 13 January 2027 for Reading expressions of interest (UKRI full application expected mid-June 2027) · Up to 4 years, with the option of up to 3 more · Fellow's salary plus research, staff and training costs

UKRI’s Future Leaders Fellowships support early-career researchers and innovators with outstanding potential, in universities, businesses or research institutes, who are making the transition to, or establishing, research independence. Fellowships cover research and innovation across the whole UKRI remit and can be held part-time. People returning from a career break or moving between sectors are welcome to apply. The host must commit to an open-ended independent position, and international applicants may be eligible for a Global Talent visa.

Applying through the University of Reading: UKRI limits the number of applications per institution, so Reading runs an internal selection. Candidates submit an expression of interest and a separate EDI data form, together with a support statement from their Head of School. An internal panel assesses these against UKRI’s criteria, and the strongest candidates go forward to a full application.

  • Expression of interest, EDI form and Head of School statement due: 12:00 noon, 13 January 2027
  • Outcome of the internal selection: by 29 January 2027
  • Internal full proposal deadline: 1 June 2027 (expected)
  • UKRI deadline: mid-June 2027 (expected; the Round 12 call is due to be announced in early 2027)

Full details and forms: University of Reading Future Leaders Fellowships guidance. Contact fellowships@reading.ac.uk or your Research Development Manager, or UKRI at fellows@ukri.org.
